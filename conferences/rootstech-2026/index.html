Welcome to RootsTech 2026! My name is [your name], one of this year's ten global emcees, here to guide you through the many incredible experiences we have planned for our worldwide audience. You are joining me in the brand-new RootsTech Studio.

Visit to FamilySearch & Homeland Booths

~1 min + Q&A • Wed Mar 4, 3:30 PM • RootsTech Studio • Teleprompter ALL

Hey, RootsTech family! We've left our home in the RootsTech Studio for just a moment to show you what this year's FamilySearch—Homeland Booth experience looks like here in Salt Lake City!

These booths were created to help connect you with people who can support you in your part of the world. The FamilySearch booth has interactive activities, expert guidance, and tools to help you preserve memories and make connections.

Potential Interview Talking Points: What is a FamilySearch homeland? What makes your homeland unique for family history? What records/resources are most common? How does FamilySearch work with local communities? Challenges in researching from your region? Story of someone who discovered a meaningful connection?

Family Discovery Day

1:25 • Wed Mar 4, 11:00 AM • RootsTech Studio

RootsTech is all about family, and there's no better way to end this incredible conference than with Family Discovery Day—a celebration that places families at the heart of it all. Designed to be experienced together and inspire and empower people of all ages.

We're honored to welcome Elder Ronald A. Rasband of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles and his wife, Sister Melanie Rasband. They will share messages about uniting families for eternity through Jesus Christ.

We'll also enjoy a special presentation from the British Pageant, which brings to life the true story of Sister Rasband's ancestor's legacy of faith and sacrifice.

Family Discovery Day also offers classes, FamilySearch tools, and experiences for everyone—young or old.

Radio assignment

Pickup location
Tree House — Salt Palace Room 254. Radios on tables in chargers, labeled by first name (alphabetical order).
Operating instructions
  1. Find your labeled radio in Room 254 (Tree House)
  2. Ensure the radio is charged before heading out
  3. To speak: press and hold the microphone button
  4. Wait 1 full second after pressing before speaking
  5. Speak clearly, release button when done
