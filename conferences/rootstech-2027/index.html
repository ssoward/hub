Open Questions to HR Operations

1. Shift length exception for leads

The Studio Producer role does not split cleanly at the recommended 4–6 hours — run-of-show continuity requires carrying the full day. Proposed as 10 hr × 2 people.

2. Emcee shifts are a rough fit

Emcees are booked in scattered 15-minute studio blocks, not contiguous shifts. A "5-hour shift" is really a container for half-day on-call availability and may read as under-utilization in reporting.

3. Pre-production days

Confirm Tue/Wed count as event shifts rather than pre-work hours. The entire B-roll package is filmed on those two days.

4. Technical crew ownership

Camera, audio, lighting, switcher, and hair & makeup are not included above — assumed to come via Media Services and vendor rather than this staffing process. Awaiting confirmation.

5. Continuity

Requesting returning 2026 language emcees where available — Korean, Japanese, ASL, Portuguese, and French coverage took significant ramp-up.
